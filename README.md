# Company AI assistant

Working name. A **startup radar**: an AI assistant that answers "Which AI startups raised money recently, what do they do, and are they hiring someone like me?", from public sources (Hacker News "Who is hiring?", SEC Form D filings, the Y Combinator directory) and free trend reports. Built like a client case: a problem, the solution, and measured results. The first slice of a bigger idea: a market radar for investment, startup ideas and job openings.

It's my learning project for forward-deployed engineer and applied AI engineer roles: LLMs, RAG, agents, evaluation and deployment. The plan and the dates are in "Learning and project roadmap" in `../plan.md`.

Status (30/10/2026): setup. The brief (who it's for, the 5 questions, the sources) comes next.
